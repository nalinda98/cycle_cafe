How the Community Supports Cyclists

Support comes not from businesses, but from people. Every contribution is voluntary and based on goodwill.

Examples of support:

  • 🏡 Free rest spaces offered by volunteer hosts
  • 💧 Water refills and simple meals from local cafés
  • 🚲 Safe bicycle parking and advice
  • ☕ Conversation, guidance, and local stories

No payments. No obligations. Just kindness.

Partner With Us

🚴 Touring Cyclist

🏡 Volunteer Host

☕ Local Café / Shop

🌱 Non-Commercial Community Initiative

CycleCafe.lk is not a hotel platform, booking service, or business marketplace. All activities are voluntary, community-driven, and free of charge.

🌱 What Is Free Stay?

Free Stay is a non-commercial, community kindness initiative by CycleCafe.lk.

We connect touring cyclists with volunteer hosts who offer one free night of rest, safety, and human connection — supporting slow travel, cultural exchange, and sustainable tourism.

🔄 How It Works

  • Request a free stay
  • Rest & recover
  • Continue your journey

🏡 For Volunteer Hosts

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 People Behind the Movement

CycleCafe.lk is not run by a company.

It is supported by cyclists, hosts, friends, and volunteers who believe in sharing space, stories, and responsibility.

Roles are flexible. Contributions are voluntary. Everyone involved is part of the same journey.
