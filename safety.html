🔐 Safety & Verification Rules

CycleCafe.lk – Free Stay & Volunteer Host Program
Read carefully before participating

🌱 Our Community Promise

CycleCafe.lk is a non-commercial, community-powered initiative. We connect touring cyclists with volunteer hosts who offer free resting space based on trust, respect, and shared responsibility.

This is not a hotel service, booking platform, or paid accommodation system.

🚴 Rules for Cyclists

To request a free stay, cyclists must agree to the following:

  • Be a touring or long-distance cyclist
  • Request only one night per location
  • Make requests in advance (no emergency demands)
  • Provide valid identification if requested
  • Respect host rules, culture, privacy, and household norms
  • Keep the space clean and leave respectfully
  • Share honest feedback after the stay
  • No alcohol, drugs, or illegal activity
  • No unregistered guests

Failure to follow these rules may result in permanent removal from the community.

🏡 Rules for Volunteer Hosts

Volunteer hosts participate freely and may withdraw at any time.

  • Offer space voluntarily (not as a business)
  • Clearly explain what space and facilities are available
  • Provide safe bicycle parking
  • Approve or decline requests without pressure
  • Share accurate location and contact details
  • Treat cyclists equally regardless of nationality or background
  • No fees, tips, or payments allowed

🟢 Verification Process

✔ Cyclists

  • Google Form registration required
  • Contact details and travel route submitted
  • Manual review by CycleCafe.lk administrators

✔ Volunteer Hosts

  • Google Form registration
  • Contact number verification
  • Location confirmation
  • Consent to community rules

Verified hosts receive the “Community Hero – Free Space for Cyclists” badge.

🚨 Safety Guidelines (Very Important)

  • CycleCafe.lk does not arrange or guarantee stays
  • All stays are agreed directly between cyclist and host
  • No emergency accommodation service is provided
  • Use local authorities for emergencies

Sri Lanka Emergency Numbers:
Police – 119
Ambulance / Fire – 110

⚖️ Legal Disclaimer

CycleCafe.lk is a community connector only. We do not own, manage, inspect, or control any accommodation.

Hosts and cyclists participate voluntarily and independently. CycleCafe.lk is not responsible for accidents, injury, loss, misconduct, or disputes.

By using this platform, you accept these terms.

🚫 Zero Tolerance Policy

  • Harassment or abuse
  • Discrimination
  • Exploitation
  • Misuse of the Free Stay program

Violations will result in permanent removal.

🤝 Shared Responsibility

This initiative survives only through honesty and respect.

  • Cyclists behave responsibly
  • Hosts act with goodwill
  • Everyone communicates clearly

Together, we are building Sri Lanka’s first cyclist kindness network 🚴‍♂️🌱
